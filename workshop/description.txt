[h1]SlaverMod[/h1]
A RE_Kenshi plugin that lets your faction own slaves, using Kenshi's own slave system: shackles, submission, supervision, escapes, rebellions, punishment, a slave trade and a slaver game start.

[b]Languages: English, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.[/b] The mod follows the game's language, or pick one in Options > MCM > SlaverMod > Language. [b]Help translate it![/b] (see below)
[i]Traduzido para português do Brasil (veja abaixo).[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Enslave from the right-click menu.[/b] Hold the right mouse button over an unconscious, chained or caged person: the selected member walks over, kneels and puts locked shackles on them. Other factions' slaves are their owners' property. Anyone who sees it treats it as kidnapping.
[*][b]Submission (0-100):[/b] Defiant, Restless, Obedient or Loyal, shown over the selected slave and saved with each save. Watched slaves slowly get used to it; cages break them faster.
[*][b]Races differ:[/b] hive drones obey, Shek resist (SlaverMod_races.ini).
[*][b]Supervision and escape:[/b] slaves left without a free member nearby lose submission and may run away. Knock them out to recapture them. Only the strong or skilled get out of their shackles; the others can only walk.
[*][b]Punish and Reward[/b] in the same menu: a beating (+submission, some damage) or a few cats.
[*][b]Freeing:[/b] slaves with 90+ submission stay in your faction; the others go home.
[*][b]Rebellion:[/b] every 7 days, if the defiant slaves are stronger than your free members, they may rise up, grab iron clubs and attack. Weak rebels stay shackled. Afterwards they flee and never trust you again.
[*][b]Slave trade window:[/b] at slave traders, buy their slaves or sell the ones you have with you; tick several and trade them all at once. Prices follow stats, race, submission and missing limbs; the list shows each one's sex. Other slavers leave your slaves alone.
[*][b]Slave caravan:[/b] every 7 days a Slave Traders caravan visits your base to buy and sell slaves, with a warning a day before. It stays a few hours and leaves.
[*][b]Reprogram skeletons (optional):[/b] an unconscious skeleton can be reprogrammed into a free member. Robotics decides the chance; a failure destroys it. Optionally it becomes the headless Skeleton No-Head MkII.
[*][b]New slaves[/b] follow and guard whoever enslaved them, only follow, or get no order and move with their squad (MCM: Follow the enslaver). Animals never enslave; enslaving them is an option (MCM: Animals can be slaves).
[*][b]Shackles[/b] are locked, weigh 3 kg (no more strength training) and stop your slaves from running. Optional: an iron neck collar instead, made by Macropod (built in, no other mod needed). Optional: enslaving needs a shackle kit, sold by slave traders.
[*][b]Cages:[/b] lock a slave in the nearest free cage from the menu, or let all of them sleep in cages at night (MCM). Slaves can be kept away from your food storage.
[*][b]Your slaves are safe from allies and neutrals:[/b] their guards, turrets and bounty hunters no longer attack them.
[*][b]Speech bubbles[/b] for slavers and slaves.
[*][b]New game start "Slave Hunters":[/b] a leader and five hunters with rusty clubs at a random slaver camp.
[*][b]In-game settings[/b] with ModConfigMenu (optional), or the commented SlaverMod.ini. Your settings are kept in SlaverMod_user.ini, so updates no longer reset them.
[/list]

[h2]Translate SlaverMod into your language[/h2]
Every text of the mod (messages, settings page, trade window, speech bubbles and the game start) is in [b]one plain text file[/b]. No coding, no tools:
[olist]
[*]Open the mod's [i]lang[/i] folder (steamapps\workshop\content\233860\3828708454\lang) and copy [i]_template.txt[/i] as your language code: de.txt, fr.txt, pl.txt, ja.txt...
[*]Fill in the right side of each line: "English text" = "your translation". Keep the spaces at the start and end. Empty stays in English, so a half-done file works.
[*]Start the game in your language: the mod picks the file by itself.
[*][url=https://github.com/Cappuccino-SKT/kenshi-slavermod/issues/new?template=translation.yml]Send it with this form[/url] (drag the file in). You'll be credited.
[/olist]
Guide: lang\_HOW_TO_TRANSLATE.txt. Chinese corrected by 石岗小学摸你穷 and Japanese by Cure, thank you! Russian and Spanish are new: native speakers, corrections are very welcome! Keep a copy of your file: Steam replaces the folder on updates.

[h2]Tip: bounties[/h2]
Slaves lose their bounties, so guards leave them alone. To hand an enslaved target in, turn that off (MCM: Slaves lose their bounties), keep them away from that faction, then free them, pick them up and turn them in.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
RE_Kenshi 0.3.x. Optional: ModConfigMenu. Tested on Kenshi 1.0.65 Steam, also with Second Life of Kenshi.

[h1]Português do Brasil[/h1]
Plugin RE_Kenshi que deixa a sua facção ter escravos usando o sistema de escravidão do próprio jogo. O mod segue o idioma do jogo; para forçar o português, use [i]Language=pt[/i] no SlaverMod.ini ou Opções > MCM > SlaverMod > Idioma.

[h2]Funções[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Escravizar pelo menu do botão direito[/b] sobre alguém desmaiado, acorrentado ou preso: o membro selecionado vai até lá e coloca grilhões trancados. Quem vê trata como sequestro.
[*][b]Submissão (0-100):[/b] Rebelde, Inquieto, Obediente ou Leal, salva em cada save. Vigiados se acostumam; a jaula quebra mais rápido.
[*][b]Raças diferentes:[/b] drones da colmeia obedecem, Shek resistem.
[*][b]Vigilância e fuga:[/b] sem um membro livre por perto, perdem submissão e podem fugir. Só os fortes ou habilidosos se livram dos grilhões; os outros só andam.
[*][b]Punir e Recompensar[/b] no mesmo menu.
[*][b]Libertar:[/b] com 90+ de submissão ficam na sua facção; os outros voltam para casa.
[*][b]Rebelião:[/b] a cada 7 dias, se os rebeldes forem mais fortes que seus membros livres.
[*][b]Janela de comércio[/b] nos traficantes: compre e venda vários de uma vez, só dos escravos que estão com você. O preço segue atributos, raça, submissão e membros faltando; a lista mostra o sexo.
[*][b]Caravana de escravos[/b] a cada 7 dias na sua base, para comprar e vender escravos, com aviso um dia antes.
[*][b]Grilhões[/b] trancados, com 3 kg (sem treino de força), que impedem correr. Opcional: uma coleira de ferro feita pelo Macropod (já inclusa) e exigir kit de grilhões para escravizar.
[*][b]Jaulas:[/b] prenda um escravo na jaula livre mais próxima pelo menu, ou faça todos dormirem nas jaulas (MCM). Dá para impedir que comam do seu estoque.
[*][b]Aliados e neutros não atacam seus escravos[/b], nem guardas, torretas ou caçadores de recompensa.
[*][b]Reprogramar esqueletos (opcional)[/b], com a opção de virarem o Skeleton No-Head MkII, sem cabeça.
[*][b]Novos escravos[/b] seguem e protegem quem os escravizou, só seguem, ou andam com o esquadrão (MCM: Seguir quem escravizou). Animais não escravizam; escravizá-los é uma opção (MCM: Animais podem ser escravos).
[*][b]Novo início "Caçadores de Escravos"[/b] num acampamento de traficantes sorteado.
[*][b]Opções dentro do jogo[/b] com o ModConfigMenu (opcional), ou no SlaverMod.ini comentado. Suas escolhas ficam em SlaverMod_user.ini, e as atualizações não apagam mais.
[/list]

[h2]Traduza para o seu idioma[/h2]
Todos os textos estão num único arquivo de texto: copie [i]lang\_template.txt[/i] como <código>.txt, preencha o lado direito de cada linha e envie pelo [url=https://github.com/Cappuccino-SKT/kenshi-slavermod/issues/new?template=translation.yml]formulário[/url].

Requisitos: RE_Kenshi 0.3.x; opcional: ModConfigMenu.
